Your stream's alerts, chat and commands - in one free app on your own PC

Castwell Toolkit brings together what streamers usually juggle several tools for: alerts and overlays, chat from every platform, commands and timers, loyalty points, song requests, a giveaway wheel, a Stream Deck plugin and a backup of your whole OBS setup. It shows up in OBS as browser sources, and everything runs on your computer - no account, no cloud, no AI.

Only in Castwell

Your whole stream setup, backed up - OBS included

Most tools back up their own settings. Castwell backs up everything your stream needs: your OBS scenes, profiles, scripts and plugins, every picture, video and sound your sources use, and Castwell itself - with the exact OBS version it all worked on. If your PC dies:

  1. Unzip Castwell Toolkit from the backup
  2. Install the same OBS from it
  3. Press Restore - your scenes, sources and media come back, reconnected, even on a new PC
Stream keys stay out unless you lock them in with a password. Nothing is re-encoded. More about backups

Linux (beta)

Untested beta: it runs, but nobody has streamed with it on Linux yet.

  1. Download the AppImage.
  2. Make it runnable: right-click > Properties > Allow executing file as program (or chmod +x Castwell-Toolkit-x86_64.AppImage).
  3. Double-click it. The dashboard opens in your browser (as its own window with Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave). If nothing happens on Ubuntu, install FUSE once: sudo apt install libfuse2t64 (22.04: libfuse2).

Your settings, sign-ins and added plugins live in ~/.local/share/castwell-toolkit. Works with normal OBS and the Flatpak one. Not on Linux yet: the icon by the clock, placing windows on monitors, installing OBS plugins and OBS updates for you (it links to their page instead). Updates: download the new AppImage and use it instead of the old one.

Getting started

  1. Download and run the installer above (Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" - press More info > Run anyway; that shows for new apps until enough people have installed them).
  2. Castwell Toolkit opens in your browser. Pick where you stream and type your channel names.
  3. Press Set up OBS for me and Add to OBS - your alerts and chat are on stream.

Signing in

Reading chat needs no sign-in. Signing in to Twitch, YouTube or Kick is optional and only lets Castwell Toolkit do what you switch on - for example answer viewers in your own chat. Sign-ins happen on each platform's own page; Castwell Toolkit never sees your passwords. TikTok's chat is read by Castwell Toolkit's small browser extension from your own TikTok LIVE page.
