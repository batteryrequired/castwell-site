Your stream's alerts, chat and commands - in one free app on your own PC

Castwell brings together what streamers usually juggle several tools for: alerts, an on-screen chat, chat commands and timers, a chat & events feed, song requests and a giveaway wheel. It shows up in OBS as browser sources, and everything runs on your computer.

Getting started

  1. Download and run the installer above (Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" - press More info > Run anyway; that shows for new apps until enough people have installed them).
  2. Castwell opens in your browser. Pick where you stream and type your channel names.
  3. Press Set up OBS for me and Add to OBS - your alerts and chat are on stream.

Signing in

Reading chat needs no sign-in. Signing in to Twitch, YouTube or Kick is optional and only lets Castwell do what you switch on - for example answer viewers in your own chat. Sign-ins happen on each platform's own page; Castwell never sees your passwords. TikTok's chat is read by Castwell's small browser extension from your own TikTok LIVE page.
