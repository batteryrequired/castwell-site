Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Castwell Toolkit is an app that runs on your own computer, together with an optional browser extension ("Castwell Toolkit for TikTok LIVE"). Castwell Toolkit has no servers. Nothing you do in Castwell Toolkit is sent to its maker or to anyone else, except the requests to Twitch, YouTube, Kick and TikTok that the features you switch on need.

What Castwell Toolkit uses, and where it stays

  • Your channel names and settings - saved in Castwell Toolkit's data folder on your computer.
  • Sign-ins (when you choose to sign in to Twitch, YouTube, Kick or Spotify): the access tokens those services give Castwell Toolkit are saved in data/secrets on your computer and used only to talk to that service. Castwell Toolkit never sees or stores your passwords.
  • Chat messages and stream events (follows, subs, gifts, Super Chats...) from your own channels: shown in Castwell Toolkit and your stream overlays while it runs, kept in memory only, never saved or sent anywhere else.
  • Files you add (alert pictures and sounds) stay in Castwell Toolkit's folder.

YouTube (Google) data

When you sign in with Google, Castwell Toolkit asks for permission to manage your YouTube account (the youtube.force-ssl scope). It uses it only to find the live chat of the stream you are broadcasting and post replies there (for example the answer to a viewer's chat command). It reads your channel's name to show you who is signed in. Castwell Toolkit does not read your videos, playlists, subscriptions or any other YouTube data, and it does not share anything it receives from Google with anyone.

Castwell Toolkit uses YouTube API Services. By using Castwell Toolkit's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Castwell Toolkit's access at any time on your Google Account's third-party connections page, or with "Sign out" in Castwell Toolkit (which also deletes the stored token).

Castwell Toolkit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Twitch and Kick

With a sign-in, Castwell Toolkit receives your channel's events (follows, subs, channel points, raids...) and can post in your chat, start raids and shoutouts, or remove spam - only the features you switch on. You can remove its access on Twitch's Connections page, on Kick's connected apps settings, or with "Sign out" in Castwell Toolkit.

The TikTok browser extension

The extension runs only on tiktok.com and on Castwell Toolkit's own page on your computer. On your TikTok LIVE page it reads the chat messages, gifts, follows and viewer count that TikTok shows there, and sends them only to Castwell Toolkit on the same computer (127.0.0.1). It can type Castwell Toolkit's replies into that chat. It stores only Castwell Toolkit's address and a connect code in the browser. It does not collect browsing history, does not read other sites, and sends nothing to anyone else.

Children

Castwell Toolkit is a tool for streamers and is not directed at children under 13.
