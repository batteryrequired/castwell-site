Backup & restore - your whole stream setup

Built for the day your PC dies: get back to streaming in three steps - unzip Castwell Toolkit from the backup, install the same OBS from it, press Restore.

  • Your whole OBS setup, not just Castwell's settings: scenes, profiles, settings, OBS scripts, filter files and transition videos - plus every picture, video and sound your sources use, wherever they are on your PC
  • Your OBS plugins come along: plugins you installed by hand into OBS's own folder are saved too, and put back on a clean install
  • The same versions come back: every backup notes the exact OBS, plugin and Castwell versions and keeps the OBS installer, so a restore puts back the setup that worked - then updates everything in a safe order (OBS, then its plugins, then Castwell)
  • Moves to a new PC: pick where the files go and Castwell reconnects every source in OBS for you
  • Big files are your call: if your media adds up to a lot it shows the big ones so you choose what goes in - nothing is shrunk or re-encoded (4K stays 4K)
  • Stream keys stay out unless you lock them in with a password; otherwise the restore lists which keys to paste
  • An optional backup after every stream (it notices when you go offline), keeping the newest few

Stream quality - the best OBS settings for your PC

Picking encoders, sizes and bitrates for several platforms is guesswork. Castwell works it out from your own hardware and your own game.

  • Scans your PC: your graphics card (its video encoders and how many it can run at once), CPU, memory and upload speed - and measures your game for a minute (CPU, graphics card, video memory) so the settings leave room for it
  • Every platform you stream to, within its own limits: Twitch, YouTube (up to 4K), Kick and TikTok - horizontal, vertical or both: Twitch Dual Format through Enhanced Broadcasting, YouTube portrait, TikTok vertical
  • As light as possible: platforms that get the same picture share one encoder; recording gets its own quality setting. Every setting says why, and it warns when video memory or upload is tight
  • Your OBS stays safe: your OBS profile and scene collection are copied to new ones called "Castwell" and the changes go there - "Back to my old profile" is one click
  • While you're live: dropped and lagged frames, "encoding overloaded", and what to change

Vertical scenes

  • Installs the free Aitum Vertical plugin for you (a second, vertical canvas in OBS)
  • Makes a vertical copy of every scene with the same sources - change one, both change - in the same order, already placed: stacked (game on top, cam below), full game with the cam in a corner, or everything scaled down. No adding sources one by one; adjust them from there
  • Linked: switching a scene in OBS switches the vertical one too - it uses Aitum Vertical's own link, so it works even when Castwell isn't open

The Live tab

Everything you touch while live, on one page.

  • Pin any card to Live: press the 📌 on a card anywhere in the app (or "Pin this page" on a plugin's page) and it works on the Live tab just like on its own page
  • Your stream info (title, game, tags) at the top, then cards you drag into place and add or remove: scene and OBS layouts, alerts (pause, skip, replay), the giveaway wheel, music, chat, sound effects, viewer counts
  • Raid out card for the end of the stream: who you follow that's live, raid in one click
  • Plugins can add their own cards

Alerts

  • Follows, subs, resubs, gift subs, gift bombs, bits, raids, channel points, hype trains, charity, YouTube Super Chats and members, Kick subs, TikTok gifts and merch orders
  • Per alert: your text ({name}, {amount}, {message}, {recipient}), picture / GIF / video, sound, length and volume
  • Variations: other looks that play from an amount ("at least"), at an exact amount, or at random
  • Gift bombs: one alert per gifted sub naming who got it, at full length - or one big alert from tiers (5, 10, 25, 50, 100...). Optional: once a bomb has played a while, other paid alerts take turns with it instead of waiting
  • Custom HTML / CSS / JavaScript for the whole alert box or one alert, like Streamlabs
  • Every sound equally loud: each sound is measured once and played at the same loudness, so a quiet clip and a loud one match - with a "keep this sound's own loudness" tick for the ones meant to stand out, like a jump scare
  • Paid alerts first, test buttons with a live preview, pause (alerts wait, then play)

Import from Streamlabs or StreamElements

  • Paste your alert box (or overlay) link - the same one you'd put in OBS - and your alerts come over: texts, pictures, sounds, volume, length, variations and your custom HTML / CSS / JS
  • The same link brings your Event List, Chat Box and Viewer Count settings too, and any Streamlabs widget link works
  • One Undo puts your old Castwell settings back. First-time setup offers it too

Event list

  • An overlay with your latest follows, subs, gifts, bits, raids, charity, Super Chats, members, TikTok gifts and merch orders - pick which, how many lines, newest on top or at the bottom, and how long each one stays
  • Font, size, colours, a stripe on each line, box and round corners, platform marks, their message, slide-in
  • Kept across restarts; test events to place it while you're offline
  • Your own design: custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript, like Streamlabs

Every platform's chat in one place

  • Twitch, YouTube, Kick and TikTok mixed together, every line marked with where it came from - in the Chat & events feed, the on-screen chat, commands, giveaways and song requests
  • Reading chat needs no sign-in (TikTok uses Castwell's own small browser extension on your LIVE page)
  • Replies go back where the viewer wrote, as you or as a bot account you made
  • Send to chat from the dashboard or the OBS dock: all platforms at once, or pick one
  • Chat & events feed with badges, emotes, filters and viewer counts per platform - pop it out or dock it in OBS

On-screen chat

  • Five themes, fonts, sizes, colours, name colours, badges and BTTV / FrankerFaceZ / 7TV emotes
  • Deleted messages, timeouts and bans disappear by themselves; leave out bots, commands or emote-only lines
  • Your own HTML / CSS / JS on top of a theme

Chat commands & timers

  • Triggers: !commands, words in chat, timers (minutes, chat lines, only while live), stream events, channel point rewards, a button, or a web link for a Stream Deck
  • Steps: chat lines, random lines, sounds on their own OBS slider (levelled like the alerts, or kept as they are), show / hide sources, switch scenes, OBS layouts and filters, recording and replay buffer, clips, shoutouts, counters, "only go on if...", waits and repeats
  • Who may use it, cooldowns, timer groups that take turns, a test mode that never posts
  • Ready-made examples, and import from a Mix It Up backup

Music

  • Spotify song requests from chat or channel points, with limits (who, how often, longest song, no explicit)
  • !song, skip from the dashboard, and a now-playing box for OBS with album art and "up next"

Giveaway wheel

  • Chat joins with a word you pick, subs can get extra chances - or spin one of your own saved lists
  • The wheel spins on stream to a fairly picked winner; open / close entries, past winners, chat announcements

Raids, shoutouts & spam shield

  • Thank-you message and Twitch shoutout when you're raided; !so for mods
  • Raid out to someone you follow who's live, with cancel during the countdown
  • Spam shield for the "best viewers on some-site" bots - subs, VIPs and mods are never touched

Points, filters & chat tools

  • Loyalty points: earned by watching and chatting, !points !top !give !gamble !duel, commands that cost points, import from Streamlabs / StreamElements
  • Chat filters: links (with !permit), caps, symbols, emotes, long messages, repeated letters and banned words - a warning first, then timeouts
  • Quotes, a viewer queue, Twitch polls and predictions
  • Steps for announcements, chat modes, ad breaks, the stream title / game and stream markers
  • Twitch ads: a warning in chat before an ad, snooze, and events when ads start and end
  • Keyboard shortcuts (Windows): run any action from a key press, even in a game

Goals, text-to-speech & more overlays

  • Shoutout player: the streamer's name, picture and one of their clips on stream; auto-shoutouts for your streamer friends
  • Goal bars for followers, subs, bits and tips, and a countdown / subathon timer that events add time to
  • Text-to-speech in your PC's own voice (free, nothing leaves your PC) for bits, tips, resub messages, a channel point reward or a command - on its own OBS source with its own volume slider
  • Tips from Streamlabs and StreamElements as alerts
  • End credits, stream labels (latest follower, top cheer, any counter), an emote wall, and media share (YouTube requests you approve first)

Stream stats

  • A viewer graph over each stream and what happened when
  • An estimate of what each stream and each month earned, with a file for your spreadsheet

Integrations

  • VTube Studio, Lumia Stream (lights), your Discord channels, web requests and LurkBait - each one a step for commands and timers
  • Lumia Stream: run your Lumia commands, set a color for a few seconds, switch a Studio scene or go back to default - Hue, Nanoleaf, LIFX, Govee and more

OBS layouts & plugins

  • Layouts: save how a scene looks (which sources are on, where they sit, how big - groups included) and switch in one click, from a command, a timer, an event or a Stream Deck
  • OBS plugins manager: install plugins from GitHub or a zip into OBS, see what you have, and keep them in the backup
  • "Set up OBS for me": Castwell finds OBS's settings and connects, and "Add to OBS" puts each overlay in your scene

Start programs

  • One click (or when Castwell starts) opens everything you stream with - OBS, VTube Studio, games, Steam and Microsoft Store apps, web pages - in order, skipping what's already open
  • Each window full size or half-screen on the monitor you pick, brought to the front
  • Waits for each program's window instead of guessing seconds, learns which program Steam and Store entries open, and shows what didn't open with Try again
  • Programs set to run as administrator (like OBS) start too

Setup & sign-ins

  • A first start that asks one question per screen - about three minutes, nothing to type but names
  • Sign-ins are optional and happen on each platform's own page; Castwell never sees your passwords. Stays connected through 12+ hour streams
  • Updates itself with its normal installer when you say so

Viewer count, merch, multistream

  • Viewer count overlay per platform and in total - with your own design (custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript) too
  • Merch orders as alerts from Fourthwall, Shopify, WooCommerce, Streamlabs and StreamElements - or from your own website: any address that lists your orders, with a check that shows the newest order before you save
  • Stream to several platforms at once: Castwell installs the free Multiple RTMP Outputs plugin for OBS

Small things that help

  • A Copy button next to every link you're meant to copy, and pages don't redraw while you're selecting text
  • Test and Import buttons in the same place on every overlay page
  • OBS plugins: install from GitHub or a zip, and remove any of them - even ones an installer put in OBS's own folder (a copy is kept)

Castwell plugins

  • Add features to Castwell itself - from a .py or .zip file or a GitHub link - on the Castwell plugins tab. You see what a plugin is, who made it, every file in it and where it came from before anything is added
  • Switch plugins on and off, remove them (a copy is kept), restart Castwell from the same page; they go into the backup
  • A plugin can add chat commands, overlays, a tab of its own and its own light on Home - how to make one, with an example to start from

Test everything

  • One button checks your accounts, OBS, every alert, your features, your backup, plugins and which of your programs are open - and says what to fix. Nothing is posted or streamed

Built to be light
