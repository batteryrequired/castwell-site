Everything Castwell Toolkit does

One free app on your own PC for the things streamers usually juggle several tools for. No account, no cloud, no AI - it shows up in OBS as browser sources and docks. This page is the full list, as of version 0.25.0.

Backup & restore - your whole stream setup

Built for the day your PC dies: get back to streaming in three steps - unzip Castwell Toolkit from the backup, install the same OBS from it, press Restore.

  • Your whole OBS setup, not just Castwell's settings: scenes, profiles, settings, OBS scripts, filter files and transition videos - plus every picture, video and sound your sources use, wherever they are on your PC
  • Your OBS plugins come along: plugins you installed by hand into OBS's own folder are saved too, and put back on a clean install
  • The same versions come back: every backup notes the exact OBS, plugin and Castwell versions and keeps the OBS installer, so a restore puts back the setup that worked - then updates everything in a safe order (OBS, then its plugins, then Castwell)
  • Moves to a new PC: pick where the files go and Castwell reconnects every source in OBS for you
  • Big files are your call: if your media adds up to a lot it shows the big ones so you choose what goes in - nothing is shrunk or re-encoded (4K stays 4K)
  • Stream keys stay out unless you lock them in with a password; otherwise the restore lists which keys to paste
  • An optional backup after every stream (it notices when you go offline), keeping the newest few

The Live tab

Everything you touch while live, on one page.

  • Your stream info (title, game, tags) at the top, then cards you drag into place and add or remove: scene and OBS layouts, alerts (pause, skip, replay), the giveaway wheel, music, chat, sound effects, viewer counts
  • Raid out card for the end of the stream: who you follow that's live, raid in one click
  • Plugins can add their own cards

Alerts

  • Follows, subs, resubs, gift subs, gift bombs, bits, raids, channel points, hype trains, charity, YouTube Super Chats and members, Kick subs, TikTok gifts and merch orders
  • Per alert: your text ({name}, {amount}, {message}, {recipient}), picture / GIF / video, sound, length and volume
  • Variations: other looks that play from an amount ("at least"), at an exact amount, or at random
  • Gift bombs: one alert per gifted sub naming who got it, at full length - or one big alert from tiers (5, 10, 25, 50, 100...). Optional: once a bomb has played a while, other paid alerts take turns with it instead of waiting
  • Custom HTML / CSS / JavaScript for the whole alert box or one alert, like Streamlabs
  • Paid alerts first, sound levelling, test buttons with a live preview, pause (alerts wait, then play)

Import from Streamlabs or StreamElements

  • Paste your alert box (or overlay) link - the same one you'd put in OBS - and your alerts come over: texts, pictures, sounds, volume, length, variations and your custom HTML / CSS / JS
  • One Undo puts your old Castwell alerts back. First-time setup offers it too

Every platform's chat in one place

  • Twitch, YouTube, Kick and TikTok mixed together, every line marked with where it came from - in the Chat & events feed, the on-screen chat, commands, giveaways and song requests
  • Reading chat needs no sign-in (TikTok uses Castwell's own small browser extension on your LIVE page)
  • Replies go back where the viewer wrote, as you or as a bot account you made
  • Send to chat from the dashboard or the OBS dock: all platforms at once, or pick one
  • Chat & events feed with badges, emotes, filters and viewer counts per platform - pop it out or dock it in OBS

On-screen chat

  • Five themes, fonts, sizes, colours, name colours, badges and BTTV / FrankerFaceZ / 7TV emotes
  • Deleted messages, timeouts and bans disappear by themselves; leave out bots, commands or emote-only lines
  • Your own HTML / CSS / JS on top of a theme

Chat commands & timers

  • Triggers: !commands, words in chat, timers (minutes, chat lines, only while live), stream events, channel point rewards, a button, or a web link for a Stream Deck
  • Steps: chat lines, random lines, sounds on their own OBS slider, show / hide sources, switch scenes, OBS layouts and filters, recording and replay buffer, clips, shoutouts, counters, "only go on if...", waits and repeats
  • Who may use it, cooldowns, timer groups that take turns, a test mode that never posts
  • Ready-made examples, and import from a Mix It Up backup

Music

  • Spotify song requests from chat or channel points, with limits (who, how often, longest song, no explicit)
  • !song, skip from the dashboard, and a now-playing box for OBS with album art and "up next"

Giveaway wheel

  • Chat joins with a word you pick, subs can get extra chances - or spin one of your own saved lists
  • The wheel spins on stream to a fairly picked winner; open / close entries, past winners, chat announcements

Raids, shoutouts & spam shield

  • Thank-you message and Twitch shoutout when you're raided; !so for mods
  • Raid out to someone you follow who's live, with cancel during the countdown
  • Spam shield for the "best viewers on some-site" bots - subs, VIPs and mods are never touched

OBS layouts & plugins

  • Layouts: save how a scene looks (which sources are on, where they sit, how big - groups included) and switch in one click, from a command, a timer, an event or a Stream Deck
  • OBS plugins manager: install plugins from GitHub or a zip into OBS, see what you have, and keep them in the backup
  • "Set up OBS for me": Castwell finds OBS's settings and connects, and "Add to OBS" puts each overlay in your scene

Start programs

  • One click (or when Castwell starts) opens everything you stream with - OBS, VTube Studio, games, Steam and Microsoft Store apps, web pages - in order, skipping what's already open
  • Each window full size or half-screen on the monitor you pick, brought to the front
  • Waits for each program's window instead of guessing seconds, learns which program Steam and Store entries open, and shows what didn't open with Try again
  • Programs set to run as administrator (like OBS) start too

Setup & sign-ins

  • A first start that asks one question per screen - about three minutes, nothing to type but names
  • Sign-ins are optional and happen on each platform's own page; Castwell never sees your passwords. Stays connected through 12+ hour streams
  • Updates itself with its normal installer when you say so

Viewer count, merch, multistream

  • Viewer count overlay per platform and in total
  • Merch orders from Fourthwall, Streamlabs, StreamElements and Shopify as alerts
  • Stream to several platforms at once: Castwell installs the free Multiple RTMP Outputs plugin for OBS

Test everything

  • One button checks your accounts, OBS, every alert, your features, your backup, plugins and which of your programs are open - and says what to fix. Nothing is posted or streamed

Built to be light

  • Runs on your own PC: nothing in the cloud, no Castwell account, nothing to pay for, no AI
  • Light on CPU, GPU and memory so your game and stream keep them; sounds play through the desktop audio OBS already captures
  • Add your own features with plugins
